l am very sorry for the late delivery, after we received the Fabric raw material from our supplier in your order, we found the raw material couldn't meet our standard quality, our supplier also explained that there was some difference in this lot.
We held a meeting of your order immediately with our production department, after some tests we decided to return the raw material and requested our supplier to make the new one with standard quality, this is the main reason why we have to delay the delivery around 25-30 days.
At this moment your understanding is very important and highly appreciated, please do trust we are not that kind of supplier who pursuit profits only, I have to keep offering you the right products with right quality, this is also the main reason that you would keep buying from us.
